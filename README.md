# Northstar Customer Recovery Agent

An agentic AI customer recovery system developed as part of the Everclick Labs Agentic Business Systems project.

> **Northstar Supply Co. is a fictional ecommerce company created as a controlled environment for designing, testing, and demonstrating agentic AI systems.**

## Project Goal

Build an AI agent capable of analyzing customer issues, determining whether company policy allows autonomous resolution, taking authorized actions, and escalating cases that require human intervention.

The system will progressively evolve from a single customer-recovery agent into a larger multi-agent business environment.

## Development Roadmap

### Stage 1 — Customer Recovery Agent
Analyze customer interactions, determine sentiment and intent, apply business rules, recommend actions, and identify cases requiring human intervention.

### Stage 2 — Customer Journey Intelligence
Add customer history, order history, memory, and proactive customer-resolution capabilities.

### Stage 3 — Agent Evaluation & Self-Improvement
Measure agent performance, identify failures, evaluate decisions, and develop mechanisms for improving agent behavior.

### Stage 4 — Business MCP Layer
Create a standardized interface allowing AI agents to securely interact with business systems and tools.

### Stage 5 — Multi-Agent Operations
Introduce specialized agents and orchestration for customer service, operations, finance, inventory, and other business functions.

### Stage 6 — Autonomous Business Simulation
Explore goal-driven AI systems capable of coordinating specialized agents and optimizing toward measurable business objectives.
