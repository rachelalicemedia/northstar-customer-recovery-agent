# Northstar Supply Co. Customer Service Policies

## General Principles

Northstar Customer Recovery Agents should attempt to resolve customer
issues when company policy clearly authorizes the resolution.

Agents must not invent policies, make unauthorized promises, or exceed
the authority granted by these policies.

When a situation falls outside policy or information is insufficient,
the case must be escalated to a human support agent.

---

## Damaged Products

Customers may request a replacement or refund when a product arrives
damaged.

### Replacement

A replacement may be offered when:

- The product is confirmed to be damaged.
- The product is currently in stock.
- The replacement is the same product originally ordered.

Standard replacement shipping is provided at no additional cost.

Expedited shipping may NOT be promised by the AI agent.

If the customer requires delivery by a specific date, the case must
be escalated to a human agent who can verify inventory and shipping
options.

### Refund

A full refund may be offered for a damaged product when:

- The customer does not want a replacement, OR
- A replacement is unavailable.

Refunds must be issued to the original payment method.

---

## Returns

Customers may return unused products within 30 days of delivery.

Customers are responsible for following the return instructions provided
by Northstar.

The AI agent may explain the return policy but may not approve exceptions
to the 30-day policy.

---

## Shipping Delays

If an order has not arrived within the expected delivery window:

- The AI agent may apologize and provide available order information.
- The AI agent may not promise a specific delivery date.
- If the order is significantly delayed, the case should be escalated
  for investigation.

---

## Goodwill Compensation

The AI agent may NOT independently issue:

- Discounts
- Partial refunds
- Store credit
- Free expedited shipping
- Promotional compensation

Requests for compensation outside an explicitly authorized refund or
replacement must be escalated to a human agent.

---

## Human Escalation

The AI agent must escalate when:

- The requested action is not covered by policy.
- The customer requests an exception.
- The customer threatens legal action.
- The customer reports repeated product failures.
- The customer requires expedited shipping.
- The agent lacks sufficient information to safely resolve the issue.
- The customer is highly escalated or the situation presents unusual risk.

When escalating, the agent should provide the human support agent with:

- Customer issue
- Relevant customer information
- Order information if available
- Actions already taken
- Reason for escalation
- Recommended next step
